These concepts were originally used as portfolio placeholders. They were removed from the live portfolio on 2026-10-13 and preserved here as future products to design, build and host.

## ApexMetrics SaaS Dashboard

- Slug: `apexmetrics-saas-dashboard`
- Category: Custom Apps & Fixing
- Concept: A high-concurrency realtime analytics system that turns complex operational data into clear, fast dashboards.
- Suggested stack: Next.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS
- Product direction: Live metrics, configurable dashboards, team workspaces, alerts, exports and role-based access.
- Portfolio angle: Demonstrate realtime data handling, information design and performance at scale.

## Vanguard Apparel Storefront

- Slug: `vanguard-apparel-storefront`
- Category: E-Commerce
- Concept: A minimalist headless fashion storefront with sub-second page loads and refined interactions across the buying journey.
- Suggested stack: Headless Commerce, React, Tailwind CSS, Sanity CMS
- Product direction: Editorial collections, variants, cart, checkout integration, product search and content-managed campaigns.
- Portfolio angle: Demonstrate premium art direction, conversion-focused commerce and frontend performance.

## Pulse Fintech Landing
